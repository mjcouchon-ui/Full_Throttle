PROBLEMS AND FIXES
1. Timing ran on real-time waits of up to 4.45 s (old lines 109 and 221), with errors adding up over a take. A long hitch or a low frame rate moves every later shot in that take, and the two builds will not hitch alike. Fix: long stretches are now counted on the game clock. "FT CLOCK level=" is logged in the first frame past each whole second of level clock (FTGameMode.cpp 1034). Each take waits for a clock line and sends FTBossNow in that same frame, so the clock lines fall on T = 1, 2, 3... of the take. Only the last 0.1 to 0.6 s before a shot is a real wait. A bare "FTFreeze 0" between two clock waits resets the waitfor window to count one more second.
2. Rocket wind-up shots hung on "waitfor fight=6." / "fight=5.". That line comes every 0.5 s from a static accumulator that is never reset (FTFlow.cpp 286-293), so the two runs could differ by up to 0.5 s (a ring 20% or 75% through its warning). Fix: clock line plus a fixed wait.
3. The first boss of the session was the filmed entrance, so first-use loads would land in the entrance and wall shots. Fix: a warm-up take with no shots before the entrance (phase 2, every attack, both helpers called and wrecked). The restart for the entrance clears all of it.
4. "waitfor FT HURT|2": on a miss the shot and everything after it in the take ran 2 s late. Fix: timeouts of 0.5 to 0.9 s, sized so a missed line still gives a frame of that attack landing, and the next shots re-synchronise on a clock line.
5. Stream wind-up had 0.1 to 0.2 s of margin that shrank with every late frame. Fix: phase 1 now starts at T=1.2 (clock line + 0.2), so the first stream shot is at 7.2 and the wind-up shot is taken on clock line T=7.
6. Their note "civilians need 6.5 s, so no take is disturbed" is wrong for takes BR, P1 and P2 (8 to 9 s). A road car spawned in lane 3 early in a take can reach the pinned car from about T=6.6 (TickCivilians 1708-1722; contact 2449-2562). It logs "FT HURT .. near=Civilian", shoves the car and throws sparks. My rough estimate is 5 to 10% per long take. A script cannot stop it, and moving the pin between lanes is worse (near-miss slow motion). Left as is; the header says what to look for.
7. Moments that moved (same names, both runs use the same script, so pairs still match):
   - Hurt rounds are fired at T=0.18 instead of 0.5.
   - Phase 2 starts at T=1.0 instead of 0.5, so the stream starts at 7.0; p2_stream is at 7.63 and p2_adds at 8.53.
   - Take BR's bursts are filmed at 3.54 / 4.03 / 4.65.
   - Road shots are now frozen stills.
8. Timeouts: FT LEVEL scene=7 and the entrance Arrive raised from 20 to 30 s; road clock waits lowered from 90 to 60 s.
9. Run line: added -ftslot=pie, so the career is on the test slot from process start (FTGameInstance.cpp 104) even if the first console line met a world without the race game mode. Budget raised from 900 to 1200 s: the sum of every timeout plus waits is about 875 s before engine start.
10. I checked that -Extra "-ftnoattract ..." (a value starting with a dash) binds correctly under "powershell -File" on this PC, using a throwaway parameter-echo script in the validate folder, since removed.
