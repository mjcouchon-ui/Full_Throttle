# combat_dense.txt : the busiest fight the test harness can stage, on game level 8 (City Center, level index 7) at
# EPIC, the autopilot driving and shooting. For before/after frames (PART 1 and the end of PART 2) and for a
# frame-time comparison (the 10 s of PART 2 between "StartFPSChart" and "StopFPSChart").
# Run it the same way on both checkouts (a new -Tag each run, the same frame size):
#   powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "K:\Full Throttle\SkyScratch\SourceArt\Jam\unreal\jam_exec.ps1"
#       -Tag <tag> -Script <this file> -Checkout <project folder> -BudgetSec 900 -Extra "-ftnoattract -ftnodynres"
# After each run, in <Checkout>\Saved\Logs\jam_exec_<tag>.log:
#   "result=timeout"   the script lost its place there (the stills after it are at the wrong moment)
#   "FTTEL "           every 2 s: ents=<things on the road> fps=<smoothed> (only printed because ft.Autopilot is 1)
#   "FTHITCH "         every frame over 40 ms
#   "FT EXEC line="    the runner's own line numbers: the timed 10 s are from "cmd=wait 10" to "cmd=StopFPSChart"
#   "FT LEVEL dump"    FTDumpLevel: a state line before and after the timed 10 s, and at the end
#   "FT BOSS state=Down" must come only after "cmd=FTWreck all" (earlier = the autopilot shot him down by itself)
#   "FT WRECK SUV|Truck|Chopper" right after "cmd=FTWreck SUV 1" / "Truck 1" / "Chopper 9": the three explosion stills
#   "FT RATING" must NOT be there (the script quits before the level is rated, so nothing is saved by this run)
#
# WHO FIRES. ft.Autopilot 1 (not FTAutoRun, which switches the car's guns and missiles off for good): the autopilot
# steers, holds the trigger all the time, and launches one missile each time the race clock passes a multiple of
# 1.7 s while missiles are in stock. A key press cannot fire while it drives. So a missile is caught from the log:
# FTPickup Crate (3 more missiles), then "fired=1" = the launch, "source=missile" = the hit.
# ORDER. FTBossNow clears the road before it brings the boss, so the boss comes first and the convoy is spawned
# round him in the same instant (the world is held with FTFreeze while the spawn commands run).
# FTSpawn takes any number; the lane of each vehicle is random (different every run), its distance is the kind's own.
# Vehicles of one kind then all steer for the same spot (every chopper to the car's right, every Snake Eyes to its
# right door, every truck to the lane left of the centre line 3 s on): more than one of those ends up inside the
# other. So PART 1 (the stills) has ONE chopper and ONE Snake Eyes; PART 2 (the load) keeps three and four.
# The car is pinned on the centre line (ft.AutoX 0) and cannot be hurt (FTInvuln 1).
# PART 1 = stills, each with the world held (FTFreeze 1 / shot / FTFreeze 0). PART 2 = the level restarted on the
# spot (so the car's turbo, missiles and clock are the same at the start of the timed part in every run), a fresh
# boss and convoy at clock 4, 10 s untouched in real time, then everything wrecked in one instant, the aftermath,
# and the boss.
# The script quits 2.3 s after the boss goes down: before the rating at 4.2 s, so nothing is saved by this run.

wait 2
FTUseTestSlot
FTCareerReset
FTQuality 3
t.FPSChart.OpenFolderOnDump 0
FTStartLevel 8 1
waitfor FT LEVEL id=|300
FTInvuln 1
ft.AutoX 0
ft.Autopilot 1
waitfor FT SPEED|60
# 4 s of scene 1 so the car is at its cruising speed and on the centre line
waitfor FT CLOCK level=4.|40

# ================================================================ PART 1: the stills
# THE CHAIRMAN on his spot (armor 220, phase 0), then the convoy, all in one held instant
FTBossNow
waitfor FT BOSS state=Arrive|10
FTFreeze 1
FTSpawn Truck 1
FTSpawn Truck 2 beside=Truck
FTSpawn SUV 5
FTSpawn Rammer 5
FTSpawn Biker 10
FTSpawn SnakeEyes 1
FTSpawn Chopper 1
FTSpawnCivilian 4
FTSpawnBarrel 6
# the whole convoy and the boss, nothing fired at it yet
shot dense_00_convoy
FTFreeze 0
# 0.65 s of the fight in real time: the car's guns (a round every 0.085 s). Their first shots come later (bikers
# 1.5 s, the boss 1.6 s, gunships 2.3 s after the spawn): they are in the stills from dense_02 on
wait 0.5
FTFreeze 1
shot dense_01_guns
FTFreeze 0
# 0.15 s later (a muzzle flash lasts less than the gap between two rounds: a second chance to have one in frame)
FTFreeze 1
shot dense_01b_guns
FTFreeze 0

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- a missile: launch, flight, impact
FTPickup Crate
# the autopilot's next launch (at most 1.7 s away); the world is held one or two frames after it
waitfor fired=1|8
FTFreeze 1
# slow motion (0.3x) for the flight: 9 real seconds from here, the held time included
FTSlowMo 9
shot dense_02_missile_launch
FTFreeze 0
# about 0.11 s of game time later: the missile some 8 m out, its trail behind it
wait 0.2
FTFreeze 1
shot dense_03_missile_flight
FTFreeze 0
# the hit (it homes on the nearest foe ahead), 0.07 s of game time old
waitfor source=missile|12
wait 0.2
FTFreeze 1
shot dense_04_missile_impact
FTFreeze 0

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- explosions: a car, a truck, a helicopter
# each one about 0.1 s of game time after the wreck (slow motion: 0.35 real seconds)
FTSlowMo 7
FTWreck SUV 1
wait 0.2
FTFreeze 1
shot dense_05_car_explosion
FTFreeze 0
wait 0.4
FTWreck Truck 1
wait 0.2
FTFreeze 1
shot dense_06_truck_explosion
FTFreeze 0
wait 0.4
# every chopper there is (one, unless the boss has called another): none is left hanging in the fireball
FTWreck Chopper 9
wait 0.2
FTFreeze 1
shot dense_07_heli_explosion
FTFreeze 0
wait 0.4

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the boss under fire
# 30 standard rounds through the gun's real hit path in one frame (30 of his 220 armor)
FTFire 30 Boss
wait 0.1
FTFreeze 1
shot dense_08_boss_under_fire
FTFreeze 0
wait 1.0

# ================================================================ PART 2: 10 s in real time, then the mass wreck
# the level again from the top, in the same world (no map load): turbo tank full, 6 missiles, clock 0, no slow
# motion left over. FTInvuln, ft.AutoX and ft.Autopilot are still on (FTInvuln is sent again in case a build
# reloads the map here)
FTStartLevel 8 1
waitfor FT LEVEL id=|300
FTInvuln 1
waitfor FT SPEED|60
waitfor FT CLOCK level=4.|40
# the frame-rate cap and vsync off for the timed part (the benchmark mode does the same)
t.MaxFPS 0
r.VSync 0
# a fresh boss, put into phase 1 at once: armor 140 of 220 = a gunship SUV called in, and his stream of shots from
# 6 s on; then the convoy again (three choppers, four Snake Eyes: the load) and three more missiles
FTBossNow
waitfor FT BOSS state=Arrive|10
FTFreeze 1
FTFire 80 Boss
FTSpawn Truck 1
FTSpawn Truck 2 beside=Truck
FTSpawn SUV 5
FTSpawn Rammer 5
FTSpawn Biker 10
FTSpawn SnakeEyes 4
FTSpawn Chopper 1
FTSpawn Chopper 2 beside=Chopper
FTSpawnCivilian 4
FTSpawnBarrel 6
FTPickup Crate
FTDumpLevel
FTFreeze 0
# ---- the timed 10 s: no still, no hold, no slow motion from the script
StartFPSChart dense
wait 10
StopFPSChart
FTDumpLevel

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- everything wrecked in one instant
# held while the commands run, so every vehicle and barrel goes up on the same frame (the boss is left alive:
# FTWreck all would wreck him too and end the level under the aftermath). Trucks and choppers first: the game's own
# 0.7 s of slow motion for a big wreck has run out by the time the world is let go.
FTFreeze 1
FTSpawnBarrel 6
FTWreck Truck 99
FTWreck Chopper 99
FTWreck SUV 99
FTWreck Rammer 99
FTWreck SnakeEyes 99
FTWreck Biker 99
FTWreck Barrel 99
FTFreeze 0
# about 0.2 s after the wrecks: every fireball open
wait 0.05
FTFreeze 1
shot dense_09_mass_wreck
FTFreeze 0
# the aftermath, 1 s and 3 s after the wrecks (not held: the game runs on)
wait 0.6
shot dense_10_after_1s
wait 1.5
shot dense_11_after_3s

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- THE CHAIRMAN goes down
# FTWreck all = the boss and whatever he called in. The wrap: hit-stop 0.22 s, then slow motion 0.22x; the still is
# 0.65 real seconds in, before the CUT! card (0.8 s). Not held: the quit must come before the rating at 4.2 s
wait 0.5
FTWreck all
wait 0.5
shot dense_12_boss_down
FTDumpLevel
wait 1
quit
