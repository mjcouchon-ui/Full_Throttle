# boss_L5.txt - MR. CHUCKLES (game level 5, Theme Park, level index 4): the whole fight, staged for before/after pairs.
# Run it the same way on both checkouts, with a new tag each time, at the full frame size (NOT -Small, see DEFEAT):
#   jam_exec.ps1 -Tag <tag> -Script boss_L5.txt -Checkout <project> -BudgetSec 900 -Extra "-ftnoattract -ftnodynres"
# After each run look in the log for: result=timeout (the script lost its place), LogFT: Error, and FTHITCH lines over
# 400 ms that are NOT next to a "Tracing Screenshot" line (a frame that long inside a take costs game time).
# Expected: "FT LEVEL id=L5 scene=1", "scene=6", "scene=7"; 9 x "FT BOSS state=Arrive armor=140 escape=80" (two of them
# are the level's own boss: the entrance, and the run-up before the defeat); "FT DAMAGE target=Boss amount=1 left=135",
# "left=92", "left=46", "left=27", then "amount=140 left=0"; one FT STUNT name="LOOP THE LOOP"; "FT BOSS state=Down";
# "FT OUTCOME wrap"; "FT RATING value=B-MOVIE"; "FTFLOW reel start, 2 shots"; "FT SCREEN name=Reel state=hold",
# "name=Dailies", "name=Paycheck"; "FT EXEC done" is NOT expected (quit ends the game first).
#
# HOW IT KEEPS THE TWO RUNS ON THE SAME MOMENTS
# - Every part is its own TAKE of a fresh boss. FTBossNow (FTFlow.cpp HarnessCmd) clears the road and the bombs,
#   puts the car on the finale line and brings a new boss on his fighting spot with his timers reset (first attack
#   T1 = 1.6 s, FTGameMode.cpp SpawnGroup case Boss), so each take is timed from its own "FT BOSS state=Arrive" line.
# - T below = seconds of game time after that Arrive line. The script's waits are real seconds; the takes run at full
#   speed, so real = game. A still is FTFreeze 1 / shot / FTFreeze 0: the world holds (time dilation 0.0001) while the
#   frame is saved. Measured today (jam_exec_bosses_before.log): a 3440x1440 shot is ONE frame of 0.5 to 0.6 s, the game
#   runs at 100 to 120 frames a second, each script line starts up to one frame late. Per still the world moves on for
#   the 0.15 s of the FTFreeze 0 line plus the wait that follows. The T values are for about 100 frames a second; at
#   30 they come 0.1 to 0.3 s later (the "windup" stills still fall before the release, the others after it).
# - No take is in slow motion except the short HURT one, and that one is followed by a level restart (StartRace
#   sets the time scale back to 1). Reason: the game counts a frame as 0.1 s at most (FTGameMode.cpp Tick, RDt), so a
#   slow motion (FTSlowMo, real seconds) lasts 0.4 s longer for every shot taken inside it and would run into the
#   next take.
# - The car is pinned on road x = 292 cm (ft.AutoX, lane 3 of 4, where every race starts): the attacks aim at the
#   car's x, and the autopilot would otherwise weave. FTAutoRun: autopilot steers, car takes no damage, never fires.
# - The boss is hurt only by "FTFire <n> Boss": n standard rounds through the real hit path (BulletHit) in one frame,
#   1 armor each on a fresh career. 140 armor: phase 1 at 66% or less (48 rounds, left=92), phase 2 at 33% or less
#   (94 rounds, left=46), the "Pop him!" line under 20% (113 rounds, left=27). FTWrapNow deals the rest in one hit.
# - The key-hint line stays on the HUD for the first 10 s of play after every level (re)start (bShowKeys). Every shot
#   is taken later than that, so it is in none of them.
#
# MR. CHUCKLES' TIMERS (FTGameMode.cpp TickBossClown): one timer, T1. It starts at 1.6 s; when it runs out one attack
# fires on that frame (today there is no wind-up state or pose) and T1 is set to the gap: 2.4 s in phase 0, 2.0 s in
# phase 1, 1.8 s in phase 2 (+0.4 s after a bomb carpet). Phase 0 and 1 order: bombs, bombs, pies, repeat. Phase 2:
# carpet, bombs, carpet, bombs, carpet, pies. A phase change sets the order back to its first attack, T1 runs on.
#   balloon bombs: 2 (+1 a phase), fall for 1.4 s (+0.12 s each further bomb) onto marks round the car (Lob "Warn")
#   bomb carpet  : three lanes of four, fall for 1.5 s (+0.1 s a lane, left to right)
#   pies         : 5 shots (a fan of 7 with a gap of 2) at 3839 cm/s, about 1.4 s to reach the car
# "windup" shots are about 0.2 to 0.3 s before T1 runs out: today they show the idle boss.
# NOT the same in two runs (random in the game): where the 2nd and later bombs land, the carpet's free lane, the pie
# fan's gap, civilian traffic, the HUD's timecode and box office.

DisableAllScreenMessages
FTUseTestSlot
FTCareerReset
FTQuality 3
wait 3

# ---- the level, from the top (scene 1, OPENING NIGHT), on the autopilot: two shots of the road. Level clock 11 and
# 14 s: the car is at full speed (it needs 6 s from the standing start) and the key hints are gone (10 s).
FTStartLevel 5
waitfor FT LEVEL id=|240
ft.AutoX 292
FTAutoRun wreck=0
waitfor FT CLOCK level=11.|60
FTFreeze 1
shot L5_boss_road_1
FTFreeze 0
waitfor FT CLOCK level=14.|60
FTFreeze 1
shot L5_boss_road_2
FTFreeze 0

# ---- warm-up take, no shots: the first boss of a session loads things, and the first FTBossNow of a race also
# puts the scene slate and ACTION! on the HUD (bombs at T=1.6, 4.0 and 8.8, pies at 6.4, three rounds into him).
FTBossNow
waitfor FT BOSS state=Arrive|10
wait 2
FTFire 3 Boss
wait 7.2

# ---- IDLE and HURT (phase 0). Idle at T=0.7: on his spot, nothing in the air (first attack when T1 = 1.6 runs out).
# Hurt: slow motion (FTSlowMo = time scale 0.3 for 2.6 real seconds, eased in at rate 12), FTFire 5 Boss at T=1.04;
# stills 0.05 s and 0.19 s of game time after the five hits, both before the first attack. The restart that follows
# ends the slow motion.
FTBossNow
waitfor FT BOSS state=Arrive|10
wait 0.5
FTFreeze 1
shot L5_boss_idle
FTFreeze 0

wait 0.3
FTFire 5 Boss
waitfor FT DAMAGE target=Boss amount=1 left=135 load=STD|3
FTFreeze 1

# ---- ENTRANCE, the real one. FTBossNow puts this boss straight on his fighting spot (FTFlow.cpp, "B.D = Car->D +
# B.Ta"), so it has no arrival. Instead: restart at scene 6 (same map, no load; the car starts just before the loop
# that ends at the scene line), drive the loop (the game logs FT STUNT name="LOOP THE LOOP" as the car leaves it,
# about 7 s after ACTION!), 4.2 s more so that the key hints are gone and the car is at full speed, then FTNextScene
# jumps the car over the finale line, where the level's own script spawns him 160 m ahead; he drops back to his spot
# 58 m ahead at up to 60 m/s (TickBoss: B.Sp = car speed + (Want - Ahead) * 1.4, clamped to 6000 cm/s).
# T = 0.2 (150 m ahead), 0.8 (112 m), 1.4 (84 m, just before the first drop at 1.6), 2.5 (on his spot, bombs falling).
# These four carry the scene slate (THE FINALE) and ACTION!, as in the real game.
FTStartLevel 5 6
waitfor FT LEVEL id=|60
FTAutoRun wreck=0
waitfor FT SPEED|30
wait 6
waitfor LOOP" points=|8
wait 4.2

waitfor FT BOSS state=Arrive|10
FTFreeze 1
shot L5_boss_enter_1
FTFreeze 0
wait 0.45
FTFreeze 1
shot L5_boss_enter_2
FTFreeze 0
wait 0.45
FTFreeze 1
shot L5_boss_enter_3
FTFreeze 0
wait 0.9
FTFreeze 1

FTFreeze 0

# ---- DEFEAT. First a restart at scene 7, so that the highlight reel holds this take only (the loop above is a
# reel clip otherwise) and the score starts from 0. The level's own boss comes with it; 7.2 s of run-up (car at full
# speed; his pies of T=6.4 are still in the air and go with him), then a fresh boss, killed at T=3.6: after his
# first bombs are down (3.0, 3.12) and their marks gone (3.37), before the next drop (4.0), so nothing hangs in the
# air (the wrap stops bombs where they are). The kill comes 10.8 s into the play, after the key hints have gone.
# FTWrapNow = his remaining armor in one hit through the gun path. The game then holds 0.22 s (hit-stop, Freeze),
# runs at 0.22x for 2.4 s (Slowmo) and ends the take when PhaseT passes 4.2 s. The CUT! card comes up 0.8 s after the
# kill on the game's clock, which counts each 0.5 s shot frame as 0.1 s, and it sits where the boss is on the screen.
# So the three defeat shots are plain shots with NO freeze and no command between them: all three are taken before
# the card, and nothing can make the script miss the FT RATING line. (At -Small a shot is much quicker, and the card
# would be up in the third.)
# moment = 0.01 s of game time after the kill (hit-stop); after_1 = 0.08 s; after_2 = 0.17 s; wrap_cut = 0.5 s after,
# with the CUT! card.
FTStartLevel 5 7
waitfor FT LEVEL id=|60
FTAutoRun wreck=0
waitfor FT SPEED|30
wait 7.2
FTBossNow
waitfor FT BOSS state=Arrive|10
FTDumpLevel
wait 3.25
FTWrapNow
shot L5_boss_defeat_moment
wait 0.25
shot L5_boss_defeat_after_1
shot L5_boss_defeat_after_2
wait 1.0
shot L5_boss_wrap_cut

# ---- THE WRAP: rating, the highlight reel, THE DAILIES, the paycheck. The reel (StartReel / TickReel): title card
# 2.2 s, the kill from the crane camera (clip from 2.6 s before the kill), the kill again from the kill camera, then
# it holds on its last frame. The reel's clock also counts a shot frame as 0.1 s, so only two moments are safe:
# reel_1 = the title card; reel_2 = the crane shot about 1 s in, the boss still alive, about 1.6 s before the kill
# (it can be 0.3 s either way); reel_3 = the held last frame (kill camera), taken off the "state=hold" line.
waitfor FT RATING value=|60
waitfor FT SCREEN name=Reel|30
wait 1.0
shot L5_boss_wrap_reel_1
wait 2.0
shot L5_boss_wrap_reel_2
waitfor FT SCREEN name=Reel state=hold|60
wait 0.3
shot L5_boss_wrap_reel_3
FTPress Continue
waitfor FT SCREEN name=Dailies|20
wait 4.5
shot L5_boss_wrap_dailies
FTPress Continue
waitfor FT SCREEN name=Paycheck|20
wait 7
shot L5_boss_wrap_paycheck
FTDumpCareer
wait 1
