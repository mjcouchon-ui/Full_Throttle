# boss_L6.txt - THE ABOMINABLE (game level 6, Ice Mountain, level index 5): the whole fight, staged for before/after pairs.
# Run it the same way on both checkouts, with a new tag each time:
#   jam_exec.ps1 -Tag <tag> -Script boss_L6.txt -Checkout <project> -BudgetSec 1200 -Extra "-ftnoattract -ftnodynres -ftslot=pie"
# (-ftslot=pie: the game starts on the test career slot, so no line of this script can ever reach the real career.)
# After each run look in the log for: result=timeout (the script lost its place), FTHITCH (a long frame inside the
# take), LogFT: Error, "FT HURT" lines with push=650 or push=-650 (the car hit traffic: the shots near it are off),
# "source=BossWall" (he touched the car: not expected).
# Expected: "FT CAREER reset slot=career_pie", then "FT BOSS state=Arrive armor=150", "yeti=alongside t=1.8" and
# "yeti=turned" TWICE (a warm-up boss, then the real one); then for the real one, in this order: "FT DAMAGE
# target=Boss ... left=140", "FT BOSS yeti=charge phase=0", "left=95", "FT BOSS yeti=charge phase=1", "left=45",
# "left=25", "left=0", "FT BOSS state=Down clock=.. fight=30 (about)", "FT OUTCOME wrap", "FT RATING".
# Eight waits on "line=- push=0 near=" must all be found.
#
# HOW IT KEEPS THE TWO RUNS ON THE SAME MOMENTS
# - ONE continuous fight (his entrance takes 5.2 s, so a fresh boss for every part would cost too much). It is put
#   back on time at every step by a log line the game prints at an exact moment of the fight:
#     "FT BOSS state=Arrive", "FT BOSS yeti=alongside", "FT BOSS yeti=turned", "FT BOSS yeti=charge", and
#     "FT HURT dmg=0.0 .. line=- push=0 near=.." = one of his attacks landing on the car. FTAutoRun makes the car
#     take no damage, but HurtPlayer still prints the line (FTGameMode.cpp HurtPlayer). "push=0" leaves out bumps
#     from traffic (those have push=650 or -650).
#   Between two such lines the script counts at most 3 s of game time by itself.
# - The fight is filmed in slow motion (FTSlowMo = time scale 0.3: a script step of 0.15 s is 0.045 s of game time,
#   "wait 1" is 0.3 s). FTSlowMo counts REAL seconds, freezes included, and cannot be cancelled, so it is asked for
#   again every few lines (15 s each time). Asking again also undoes a near miss's own slow motion (traffic passing
#   the car sets the scale to 0.35 until the next FTSlowMo), which is why the two long waits are cut in two.
#   The last FTSlowMo leaves about 7 s at the kill, so the whole wrap (4.2 real seconds) runs at the kill's own scale
#   of 0.22 and the three defeat stills do not depend on how long a frame takes to save.
# - A still is FTFreeze 1 / shot / FTFreeze 0 (the world holds at time dilation 0.0001 while the frame is saved).
#   The HUD's clock is REAL time and runs on in a freeze: a shout (ICE!, AVALANCHE! ...) shows for 1.0 real second,
#   a caption for 3.0, the CUT! card from 0.8 after the kill. Every still is placed at least 0.15 real seconds (most
#   of them 0.25 or more) away from those edges, so the same words are on screen in both runs.
# - The car is pinned with ft.AutoX (the autopilot would otherwise weave, and his attacks aim at the car's x):
#   x = 585 cm (between lanes 3 and 4) up to the summon, so that the car is surely right of the centre line when he
#   spawns (he comes past on the other side: SpawnGroup case Boss, "E.X = (Car->X > 0 ? -1 : 1) * 78 * XU" = on the
#   LEFT); then, after the first entrance still, x = 15 cm (the centre line, between lanes 2 and 3, clear of the
#   traffic in both). FTAutoRun: autopilot steers, car takes no damage, car never fires.
# - The boss is hurt only by "FTFire <n> Boss": n standard rounds through the real hit path in one frame, 1 armor
#   each on a fresh career. 150 armor: 10 rounds -> 140 (hurt shots, still phase 0), 45 -> 95 (phase 1 is 66% or
#   less = 99), 50 -> 45 (phase 2 is 33% or less = 49), 20 -> 25 (under 20%: the director's "He's slowing down!").
#   FTWrapNow deals the rest in one hit. Each FTFire is sent right after an attack has landed, at least 0.7 s of game
#   time before his next attack is chosen, because a phase change restarts his attack order.
#
# THE ABOMINABLE'S TIMERS (FTGameMode.cpp TickBossYeti, SpawnGroup case Boss; s = game seconds after he has turned)
#   entrance: spawns 26 m BEHIND the car in the next lane, comes past on a 4.5 s clock (level with the car at 1.83 s:
#     "yeti=alongside"), reaches 40 m ahead ("yeti=turned"), spins round in 0.7 s, roars: s = 0.
#   attack timer T1: 1.2 s at s = 0; each time it runs out one attack starts and T1 = the gap: 2.4 s in phase 0,
#     2.0 s in phase 1, 1.7 s in phase 2 (+0.5 s after an avalanche). It keeps running while he charges.
#     phase 0 order: snowballs, ground pound.  phase 1 and 2: snowballs, ground pound, avalanche.
#     snowballs  : 2 (+1 a phase), no wind-up, in the air 1.2 s (+0.12 s each further ball), the first onto the car
#     ground pound: the car's lane is warned for 1.0 s (0.85 s from phase 1), then ice spikes for 0.45 s; phase 2: two lanes
#     avalanche  : a wall of snow with one gap (a random lane), warned 0.8 s where he stands, then it rolls at the car
#                  at 3976 cm/s (4387 in phase 2): about 1.1 to 1.25 s from his spot 49 m ahead
#   charge timer T3: 8.0 s at s = 0. The charge lasts 2.4 s: 0.8 s leaning in and glowing (wind-up), then he closes
#     to about 20 m; then T3 = 9 s (phase 0), 7 s (phase 1), 5.5 s (phase 2).
#   This script's fight, in s: snowballs 1.2 (land 2.4), pound 3.6 (spikes 4.6), [FTFire 10 at 4.95], snowballs 6.0,
#   CHARGE 8.0-10.4 (a pound at 8.4), snowballs 10.8 (land 12.0), [FTFire 45 at 12.1 = phase 1], three snowballs 13.2
#   (land 14.4), pound 15.2 (spikes 16.05), AVALANCHE 17.2 (rolls from 18.0, at the car 19.12 to 19.24), second charge
#   19.4-21.8, three snowballs 19.7 (land 20.9), [FTFire 50 at 21.0 = phase 2], four snowballs 21.7 (land 22.9),
#   pound on two lanes 23.4 (spikes 24.25), [FTFire 20 at 24.35], KILL at 24.9: the pound is over (24.7), the next
#   avalanche would start at 25.1 and the next charge at 27.3, so the road is empty when the wrap stops everything.
# "windup" shots of the snowballs and the pound: today nothing shows before the throw, and the pound's only warning
# is the lane mark; they are there to catch a wind-up pose if the overhaul adds one.

# ---- the test career slot, a fresh career, Epic quality (all before the level starts). FTUseTestSlot is sent twice
# and each one is waited for, so FTCareerReset can only ever act on career_pie; the third waitfor proves it did.
FTUseTestSlot
waitfor FT SAVE slot=career_pie reason=UseTestSlot|20
FTUseTestSlot
waitfor FT SAVE slot=career_pie reason=UseTestSlot|20
FTCareerReset
waitfor FT CAREER reset slot=career_pie|10
FTQuality 3
ft.AutoX 585
wait 2

# ---- the level, from the top (scene 1, FIRST TRACKS), on the autopilot: two shots of the road
FTStartLevel 6
waitfor FT LEVEL id=|240
FTAutoRun wreck=0
waitfor FT SPEED|60
waitfor FT CLOCK level=3.|90
FTFreeze 1
shot L6_boss_road_1
FTFreeze 0
waitfor FT CLOCK level=8.|60
FTFreeze 1
shot L6_boss_road_2
FTFreeze 0
# the key hints leave the screen 10 s after ACTION; the half pipe starts near 23 s
waitfor FT CLOCK level=11.|60
FTDumpLevel

# ---- warm-up boss, real time, no shots: the first yeti of a session loads and compiles what the fight draws.
# 55 rounds at once put him in phase 1 from his first step: three snowballs land 7.6 s after the Arrive line, spikes
# at 9.25, an avalanche rolls from 11.2 and reaches the car near 12.4. He is replaced before his first charge (13.2).
# Nothing is timed off this one.

waitfor FT BOSS state=Arrive|10
FTFire 55 Boss
wait 12.2
