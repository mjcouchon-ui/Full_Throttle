# Power-ups: concept approval

Owner: Justin. Date: 2026-11-14. Reference set approved in chat: existing game art and the supplied power-up brief. Justin approved **R1 and M2** in chat: green mechanic's toolbox and orange three-missile carrier.

## Target look

Memorable 1980s spy-car props with deliberate, chunky construction. Read the object first, its orange or green identity second, and a single oversized missile or wrench motif third. Preserve the KESTREL's angular planes, navy structural breaks, restrained neon accents, and the world's semi-cel shading. Use broad painted surfaces, sparse substantial fittings, strong silhouette cutouts, and thin purple/navy contour ink. Green repair bodies must remain green beside their ivory symbol; the observed night pickup currently loses its symbol in a white luminous face.

Design at the actual gameplay scale: roughly 100-110 cm bodies within the unchanged collection footprint. Treat visible missiles and tools as exaggerated visual language, preserving all existing rewards, interaction timing, and hitboxes. Small-scale readability and the 100 m requirement remain to be tested in-engine.

| C: Stunt gadgets | M3: one diagonal rocket nested into an angular orange cassette | R3: folding green caddy with an angled spanner forming a low arch | Expressive angled forms; must verify silhouette through rotation |

Mixing missile and repair directions is allowed. Selection should prioritize legible function, distinct silhouette, and visual continuity with the car.

## Shared treatment proposed for every direction

- Orange = missiles; green = repair. Do not invent extra pickup types.
- Up to four material sections. Broad paint, dark structural/guard surfaces, ivory metal/tool accents, localized emissive icon/trim. Triangles at or below the brief's roughly 5,000 limit.
- Keep emissive light on the symbol or selected seams. Do not use full-white glowing panels to carry the model.
- Slow, shallow idle bob and restrained turn/yaw can be tested after form approval. Keep visual animation within the existing pickup interaction area. Do not animate the logical spawn position.
- A brief local graphic collection accent is a later proposal. Beacons and bursts need their own measured cost and Low-quality reduction. Concept illustration does not validate these effects.
- No shared cel changes, gameplay changes, additional lights, paid sources, or copied external designs are included in this concept stage.
